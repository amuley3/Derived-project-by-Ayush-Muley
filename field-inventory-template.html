F2 · Task and Data Plan

This table explains the fields used in my Community Car Meet Registration form. It shows why each value is needed, whether it is required, and how the information would be used.

Visible label / question Why necessary now? Required? Native control/type Submitted name/value Autocomplete/inputmode Constraint/instruction Sensitivity and minimization decision
Name Identifies the attendee in a real event. Yes Text input name: attendee's name autocomplete="name" Cannot be blank. Personal information. Use fictional data only.
Email Address Could be used to contact the attendee about the event. Yes Email input email: entered email autocomplete="email" Required; browser checks basic email format. Personal information. Use a fictional email address.
How will you attend? Helps the organizer understand whether the person is visiting or displaying a car. Yes Select menu attendance: visitor, display, or both Not needed The user must choose an option. Low sensitivity. Used for event planning.
Car Make Identifies the make of the car that may be displayed. No Text input car_make: entered car make Not needed Optional text field. Low sensitivity. Vehicle information only.
Car Model Provides more information about the displayed car. No Text input car_model: entered car model Not needed Optional text field. Low sensitivity. Vehicle information only.
Car Year Shows the year the car was made. No Number input car_year: entered year Not needed Minimum 1900 and maximum 2027. Low sensitivity. A vehicle year does not require a registration plate or VIN.
Would you like to display your car? - Yes Records that the attendee wants to display a car. No Radio button display_car: yes Not needed Shares the display_car name with the No option. Low sensitivity. Event preference.
Would you like to display your car? - No Records that the attendee does not want to display a car. No Radio button display_car: no Not needed Shares the display_car name with the Yes option. Low sensitivity. Event preference.
Tell us about your car Lets the attendee provide a short description of the vehicle. No Textarea car_notes: entered description Not needed Maximum 250 characters. Optional text. Users should not include private information.
Interested in future car events? Shows whether the attendee is interested in future events. No Checkbox future_events: yes when checked Not needed Optional. An unchecked checkbox is not submitted. Low sensitivity. Optional event preference.
